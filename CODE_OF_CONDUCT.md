# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in dsui a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing opinions and experiences, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insults, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with them. Reports may be made privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will respect the reporter’s privacy and apply consequences proportionate to impact and recurrence.
